Your account

An optional public username for this wallet, so people creating elections can add you by name. It is stored with your wallet address and plays no part in voting.

1. Register (linked to your wallet)

Generates a secret identity in this browser and signs its public commitment with MetaMask. It is published to Nostr and a relayer puts it on-chain, so your wallet needs no gas and can stay on whatever network it is on (Ethereum, for example). The signature carries no vote.

2. Vote (anonymous)

Needs no wallet. Your browser proves "I hold one of the registered identities" without saying which, and publishes only the proof to Nostr under a throwaway key, for any relayer to submit.
