1. Register (linked to your wallet)

Generates a secret identity in this browser and signs its public commitment with MetaMask. It is published to Nostr and a relayer puts it on-chain, so your wallet needs no gas. The signature carries no vote.

2. Vote (anonymous)

Needs no wallet. Your browser proves "I hold one of the registered identities" without saying which, and publishes only the proof to Nostr under a throwaway key, for any relayer to submit.
