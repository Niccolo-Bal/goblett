    How a vote reaches Arc

    Registration and voting take different routes. Each hop sees only part of the picture, and no hop sees both your wallet and your ballot.

    Registration

    Once, with your wallet. Signing costs nothing and works on any network.

    Voting

    From the browser, with no wallet and no gas.

    1. Your device

      RegistrationYour wallet signs a commitment to a secret kept in this browser.

      VotingYour browser proves it holds one registered secret, without saying which.

    2. OHTTP relay

      RegistrationSees your IP address. The message stays encrypted.

      VotingSees your IP address. The ballot stays encrypted.

    3. Nostr and a relayer

      RegistrationSee your wallet and commitment.

      VotingSee the ballot and proof, not who sent them.

    4. Arc

      RegistrationRecords that your wallet registered.

      VotingCounts the ballot. Its nullifier blocks a second vote.

    Known limits

    Your secret lives in your browser
    It is the only way to vote. Back it up from the election page; anyone holding it can vote as you.
    The tally is public while voting runs
    Counts update as votes land. Who cast them does not appear anywhere.
    We run today's OHTTP relay
    Independent relays would remove that last point of trust. They are next on the roadmap.

    Relayers earn a bounty per vote

    A relayer listens on Nostr, checks each proof and submits it. The election refunds its gas plus the bounty. Changing a ballot breaks its proof, so no relayer has to be trusted.
