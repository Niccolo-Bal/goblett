    How does this stay private?

    Your browser generates a secret identity and MetaMask signs its public commitment. The signature is published to Nostr and a relayer puts it on-chain, so your wallet pays no gas and can stay on whatever network it is on (Ethereum, for example). The signature carries no vote.

    Your voter key

    This key is the only way to cast your vote. Save a copy in case you clear this browser or switch devices. Keep it private: anyone who has it can vote as you.

          How does this work?
          
    
            Your browser proves "I hold one of the registered voter keys" without saying which, and publishes only
            that proof to Nostr under a throwaway key, for any relayer to submit. No wallet is involved.
