How a vote reaches Arc

Registering and voting take different routes, so nothing along the way sees both your wallet and your ballot.

Registration

You do this once, with your wallet. Signing is free.

Voting

Done in the browser. You don't need a wallet or gas.

  1. Your device

    RegistrationYour wallet signs a commitment to a secret that stays in this browser.

    VotingYour browser proves it holds a registered secret without revealing which one. This takes 10 to 30 seconds.

  2. OHTTP relay

    RegistrationSees your IP address but can't read the message.

    VotingSees your IP address but can't read the ballot.

  3. Nostr and a relayer

    RegistrationSee your wallet and your commitment.

    VotingSee the ballot and its proof, but not who sent them.

  4. Arc

    RegistrationRecords that your wallet registered.

    VotingCounts the ballot. A nullifier stops anyone voting twice.

Before you start

Voting is ranked choice
The contract runs the instant runoff itself, so anyone who checks gets the same winner.
An election can't be edited
The candidates, voter list and schedule are fixed when you deploy. Not even you can change them.
You pay the gas, in USDC
You fund the relayers when you deploy. Whatever they don't spend comes back after voting ends.
Your secret lives in your browser
It is the only way to vote, and anyone who has it can vote as you. Back it up from the election page.
The tally is public during voting
Counts update as votes come in. Who cast them is never shown.
We run the OHTTP relay for now
That makes us a point of trust. Independent relays are next on the roadmap.
