Why Goblett GitHub Sign in

Most votes make you choose: private, or provable.

Goblett gives you both.

  1. 01

    A real secret ballot

    Nobody can see how you voted. Not the other voters, not the relayers, not even the organiser.

  2. 02

    A count anyone can check

    Every ballot is counted on Arc in public. No spreadsheet to trust, no "the results are in" email.

  3. 03

    One person, one vote

    Only people on the voter list can vote, each exactly once. The voters and choices are locked when the election starts.

  4. 04

    Free for voters

    No gas, no crypto needed. Sign in with email or a wallet, vote from the browser.
