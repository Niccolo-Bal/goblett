How this works

Goblett keeps the signing key in MetaMask. The ballot asks MetaMask to sign, then a relayer submits that signature on the chain.

Signing in

Connect MetaMask and sign a message that names your wallet. The page checks that the signature recovers to that same address. The key never leaves the wallet.

Who can vote

Each election has a whitelist on the contract. The relayer reads that list from the chain and the ballot shows only the elections your wallet is on.

Ways to vote

A yes or no election has two options. A choose-one election asks you to pick one candidate out of several, such as four names. A ranked-choice election asks you to click candidates in the order you prefer. Click a ranked candidate again to take them off the ranking.

Yes or no answers are the votes the current contract counts. Choose-one and ranked-choice answers stay on the ballot so those formats can be signed the same way.

Sending the vote

Submit signs the yes or no selections. The relayer checks the signature and the whitelist, then sends the transaction. You do not pay that gas from MetaMask.

Nostr key

Every person who opens Goblett uses this same Nostr key. The relayer signs its notes with it.

Election cost

This reads the current gas price, then runs transformElectionCost. That function is the place for the cost formula. Until a formula is added, it returns nothing.
